# Welcome to the LLaMA Pro Wiki!

Welcome to **LLaMA Pro - Distributed AI Engine**. We've taken the robust foundation of raw C/C++ LLM inference powered by `llama.cpp` and wrapped it into a sleek, feature-rich desktop application designed for power users, developers, and AI enthusiasts.

Whether you're looking to run powerful open-source models natively on your hardware, distribute workloads across multiple machines, or fine-tune models directly on your desktop, LLaMA Pro has you covered.

## 🌟 Key Highlights

- **Local LoRA Training:** Fine-tune models directly on your machine without needing external cloud services.
- **Compute Pool:** Utilize a decentralized peer-to-peer network to distribute compute loads seamlessly.
- **Real-time Telemetry:** Monitor hardware usage, system performance, and inference metrics at a glance.
- **Advanced Prompt Engineering Controls:** Tweak generation parameters, system prompts, and formatting on the fly.
- **Stunning UI with Multiple Themes:** Enjoy a rich, responsive interface with multiple aesthetic options—including our brand new **Dark City Neon Cyberpunk Hacker Console** theme!
- **Dynamic Model Loading:** Hot-swap local `.gguf` models or connect to online API-powered models instantly using our router mode.
